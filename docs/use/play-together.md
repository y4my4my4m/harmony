# Play together

Play together runs small multiplayer activities, such as the drawing game DrawinPhone, right inside a conversation. Everyone in the DM, group, text channel or call can join; nobody needs to be in a voice call to play.

## Start an activity

1. Open a DM, a group or a text channel.
2. In a text channel, select **Activities** (the popcorn) in the header, on a phone too. In a DM or a group, open **More options** (**⋮**) in the header and select **Activities**.
3. Pick an activity.

The activity opens in a panel above the messages, and a notice in the conversation tells everyone you started it, with a **Join** button.

In a voice call, select **Activities** in the call controls and pick a game under **Play**. The activity takes the call's stage, the way a shared screen does, with the call's people in a strip beneath it.

Only one activity runs in a conversation or channel at a time. If one is already running, starting another joins the running one.

Activities your instance installed (marked **Third-party**) play in Harmony on a computer. On a phone or tablet the picker marks them **Computer only**; the built-in activities play everywhere.

## Join

Select **Join** on the notice in the conversation, or on the banner above the messages that says who is playing. In a call, join from the banner on the stage. You need to be in the call to join a call's activity. In a text channel you need to be allowed to send messages there, and a member timed out in the server cannot play in its channels: the banner and the notice say why instead of offering **Join**.

## The panel

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Drag the bottom edge | Makes the docked panel taller or shorter. The height is kept on this device. |
| Expand | Covers the messages with the activity. Select it again to dock the panel. |
| End for everyone | Ends the activity for every player. Shown to whoever started it, and in a channel to members with **Manage messages**. |
| Leave | Closes the activity for you. Others keep playing. |

The activity keeps running when you open another conversation: a card in the corner shows it, with **Return** and **Leave**.

When the last player selects **Leave**, the activity ends at once. When the last player only lost the connection, it waits a minute for them to come back. The notice in the conversation then shows how long it ran and how many played.

## DrawinPhone

Everyone writes a prompt. Each prompt then passes around the group: the next player draws it, the one after describes the drawing, and so on until every chain has gone around. At the end, the album replays every chain, drawing by drawing, and everyone can react and pick favourites.

The host (whoever started the game, or the next player if they leave) picks the mode and timers in the lobby. When a timer runs out, whatever you have drawn or written is sent.

## Privacy

Activities run in a sandbox. An activity sees the names and avatars of the people playing and what they send in the game, nothing else: not your account, your messages or other conversations. It cannot open windows, download files or navigate the app. Saving an image from an activity goes through Harmony's own download, only as a picture, only right after you click in the activity, and at most 20 per game.

What you type or draw in an activity goes to the activity and the other players. Never type a password into an activity: Harmony never asks for one there.

Activities your instance's admins installed from outside Harmony show a **Third-party** label in the picker and on the Join banner, and a line above the game while you play.

Activities are available only to accounts on this instance, and only in servers this instance hosts. Your instance's admins choose which activities are offered.
